# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community harassment-free for everyone.
We pledge to act in ways that support an open, welcoming, inclusive, healthy
community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give and accept constructive feedback
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them
- Focus on what is best for project and community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, harmful, or disruptive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, wiki edits, commits, and official project
communication.

It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public
spaces.

## Enforcement

Abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers through the repository issue tracker or another listed
contact method.

Reports will be reviewed fairly. Maintainers are expected to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.
